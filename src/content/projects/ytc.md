## The idea

Click once on a YouTube video, get a transcript you can actually use. No caption hunting, no copy-paste from a panel that was not built for reading.

## Why I built it

I wanted the text, not the player UI. A tiny extension plus a backend was enough to prove it.

## How it works

The extension talked to a Rails app. Profiles, config, the usual amount of machinery for something that started as one button. Fly.io held the backend for a while.

## What I learned

A tool with a server has a heartbeat. If I am not using it weekly, I am paying for a ghost. Sunsetting is a status, not a failure.

## Current state

Sunsetted. No demo. The idea still makes sense. The instance does not.
